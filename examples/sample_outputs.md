9. The Company shall make payment for the Cashless facility to the authorized amount, directly to the Network Provider (Section 11.5.d).

## 8. Plus Benefit

**Q:** What is the Plus Benefit? Explain in detail.

`Sources: 0 (-0.89), 4.9 (-1.71), 4.10 (-2.61), 4.4.1 (-4.13), 13.2 (-4.53), 4.4.1 (-5.39), 9 (-5.77), 3 (-7.43)`<br>
`Context tokens: 1323`

**A:** The Plus Benefit is an additional coverage offered under the Optima Secure insurance policy. It provides an additional coverage amount equivalent to 100% of the Base Sum Insured. This benefit is available only if the policy is renewed and the due premium is received within the Grace Period (Section 4.4.1, Note ii).

In case the policy is issued on an individual basis, the Plus Benefit is added and available individually to the Insured Person. On the other hand, if the policy is on a floater basis, the Plus Benefit is added and available to all Family Members on a floater basis (Section 4.4.1, Note i).

If the Insured Persons in the expiring policy are covered on an individual basis and there is an accumulated Plus Benefit for such Insured Persons under the expiring policy, and such expiring policy has been Renewed on a floater policy basis, then the Plus Benefit to be carried forward for credit in such Renewed Policy shall be the lowest one that is applicable among all the Insured Persons (Section 4.4.1, Note iii).

In case of floater policies where Insured Persons Renew their expiring policy by splitting the Sum Insured into two or more floater policies/individual policies or in cases where the Policy is split due to the child attaining the Age of 25 years, the Plus Benefit of the expiring policy shall be apportioned to such Renewed Policies in the proportion of the Sum Insured of each Renewed Policy (Section 4.4.1, Note v).

The Plus Benefit is included in the sequence of utilization of the Sum Insured, following the Base Sum Insured. A single claim in a Policy Year cannot exceed the sum of Basic Sum Insured, Cumulative Bonus (if applicable), Plus Benefit (if applicable) and Secure Benefit (if applicable) (Section 13.2).

Lastly, it is important to note that the premium amount paid under this Policy qualifies for a deduction under Section 80D of the Income Tax Act (Section 9).
